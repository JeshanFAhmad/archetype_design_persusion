## My audience, values, and intent

My goal is for my design projects to be seen by my fellow students and teachers. I love being creative and having fun, and I want others to see that in my work.

My personality traits that the AI picked up on are "Creator" since I love creating something, and "Jester" because I would like others to be entertained by whatever I create. I personally agree with these suggestions because they fit with exactly what I like to be.

## Applying persuasion to my personal brand

Liking: I can include parts of my creative process and take a more playful and friendly tone in writing. This will allow the classmates and teachers to relate better to the creator of this work and form their own opinion on my style.

Authority: I can provide class projects as examples of my creative skills and tell about my experience with them. This way, my audience will get proof of my skills without being misled by my false authority.

Unity: I can describe my work as a part of our collective learning and creation process in class. This will tie my web page to our common identity. I will make sure that people can hold different opinions and still be part of us.

Commitment and consistency: I can declare my commitment to creativity and fun and support it by choosing certain examples and words that align with these values. This way, my web page will gain its internal consistency. It does not require the visitors to agree with me or do anything.
