## Management workflow

1. Read the [current assignment](../../assignment.md), [team exceptions](../CURRENT_REQUIREMENTS.md), and [verified progress](../../PROGRESS.md).
2. Coordinate issue ownership, exact paths, and reviewers. Do not silently reassign unallocated persuasion pages.
3. Inspect the PR's actual head commit, files, sources, examples, and base branch. Keep submitted, reviewed, merged, fork-synced, and Canvas-submitted states separate.
4. Request substantive teammate review. Record agent or bot reviews honestly; they do not establish another student's review.
5. Resolve conflicts by preserving each contributor's content and authorship. Do not force-push over teammates or discard their commits to make branches match.
6. Check topic indexes and About-page links. The generic assignment requires two original designs per archetype, two historical examples per style, concrete persuasion examples, and About contribution records.
7. Confirm the current Canvas submission route and Part 1 scope. No Canvas receipt is established merely by a merge or a role-status report.

## Questions to guide a review

- What did this contributor actually change, and which requirement does it satisfy?
- Which claims have supporting sources, and which are the contributor's interpretation?
- Are design examples original, labeled, readable, and connected to a named style and persuasion principle?
- What remains incomplete, and who has agreed to own it?
- Does the latest commit preserve other people's work?
- Is the next action a content revision, teammate review, merge, fork sync, or confirmed submission?
