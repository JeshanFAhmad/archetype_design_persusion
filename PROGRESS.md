## What counts as ready for review

- Assigned pages have real explanations, examples, web-design applications, and sources in place of TODO text.
- The contributor has checked factual claims, image credit/alternative text where applicable, rendered Markdown, and links.
- The pull request targets `ahmet360/archetype_design_persusion:main` and includes the assigned pages.
- The contributor posts the PR link and any blockers in the group chat.
- Ahmet reviews the changes and either requests fixes or merges the PR. Submission and approval are separate statuses; do not mark work reviewed merely because a PR exists.

Team members work in their own forks. Ahmet uses a topic branch in this repository, as explained in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
