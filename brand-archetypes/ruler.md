## Overview

The Ruler is a brand archetype of brands that embody traits of responsibility, competence, and control. Among the examples of such brands given in a study on archetypes of brands, one can mention Mercedes-Benz. In other words, the use of it may enable people to feel competent in handling significant responsibilities. It is a method of brand analysis.

## Key characteristics

- Present information in a clear, organized way.
- Emphasize reliability, responsibility, or expertise.
- Help users feel confident about making decisions.

These are ways to apply the archetype, not a guarantee that a certain visual style will make people trust a brand.

## Brand or website example

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class. The company’s S-Class page highlights its safety features, engineering and the customization options. Kamlot and Calmon identify Mercedes-Benz as an instance of the Ruler archetype as well. My own interpretation is that such themes can go with the Ruler archetype. However, this doesn’t mean that Mercedes-Benz officially claims to be a Ruler brand.

## Application to web design

Whereas the website is used in making a critical decision, then the Ruler concept may apply where the website has clear organization, navigation and comparison elements. For instance, in a page dedicated to cars, the website should have separate sections where safety features, pricing and customizations are provided for easy location.
