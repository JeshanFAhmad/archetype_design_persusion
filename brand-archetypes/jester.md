## Overview

The Jester archetype is an example of an archetype for brands that represents joy and relationship. According to Kamlot and Calmon in their research on brand archetypes, the Jester is placed in the category of “belonging and enjoyment.” To put it simply, a brand that is a Jester type uses humor to make customers have a good time or interact with other people.

## Key characteristics

Use jokes, playful names, or surprising wording.
Invite people to share an enjoyable experience.
Keep the tone informal and lively.
The humor should not make important information hard to understand or make people feel like the joke is at their expense.

## Brand or website example

Ben & Jerry’s. On its “About Us” page, the company adopts a humorous tone through headings such as “It’s Greekin’ Good!” when talking about its Greek yogurt. My opinion: This humor can be classified as a light brand voice, and it doesn’t show that Ben & Jerry’s considers itself to be a Jester brand.
This is shown in a research paper, where in a survey conducted on the Brazilian beer brand Skol, the Jester was the archetype that best described the brand according to the survey respondents, said Kamlot and Calmon in “Archetypes in the management of a brand”.

## Application to web design

A website can incorporate some humorous headings or labels for flavors so that visiting is fun. However, information about the products, prices, buttons and directions need to be very clear. For instance, the website can have playful fonts or unexpected layouts.
