## Overview

The Hero archetype is driven by a desire to **overcome adversity**. Heroes prove their worth by facing obstacles, pushing through hardship and coming out stronger on the other side. They believe that anything can be beaten with enough effort and resolve. Hero brands promise their audience the strength to rise to a challenge and win.

## Key characteristics

- **Disciplined and determined:** Hero brands value hard work, persistence and training. They celebrate the effort it takes to improve and the refusal to give up.
- **Competitive and confident:** They are strong and self-assured, and they focus on winning. They speak with conviction and set high standards for themselves and their audience.

You can often spot a Hero brand by the way it makes **the customer the hero**. The product is a tool that helps the user succeed, but the person is the one who does the hard work and earns the victory.

## Brand or website example

**[Nike](https://www.nike.com/)**

Nike is a clear example of the Hero archetype. Its website is built around the **"Just Do It"** message. Banners, campaign headlines and calls to action are short and motivational, and they push the visitor to stop hesitating and take action. The copy rarely sells the shoe itself. Instead it speaks to the customer's own drive, which casts the visitor as the hero and Nike as the gear that helps them overcome whatever stands in their way.

## Application to web design

- **Imagery:** Use dynamic action photography and video of people mid-effort, such as sweating, straining or crossing the finish line. The visuals should capture both the struggle and the triumph.
- **Typography:** Use heavy, condensed, uppercase sans-serif headlines. Large, tight type feels powerful and urgent, and it gives short slogans maximum impact.
- **Copy and CTAs:** Write in the imperative with short, motivational phrases ("Start now", "Push further", "Join the challenge"). Calls to action should speak to the user as the one taking action, which reinforces their role as the hero.
