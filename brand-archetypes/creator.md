## Overview

Creator is a brand archetype that involves creation or innovation. In their analysis of brand archetypes, Kamlot & Calmon state that the Creator brand archetype has an affinity for creating or innovating and give the example of LEGO. Basically, Creator design thinking involves using design to help people make an idea tangible or tangible. The concept is an approach to analyzing a brand rather than the choice of a brand archetype.

## Key characteristics

- Encourage people to make or customize something.
- Give them tools, examples, or steps to help them turn an idea into a result.
- Let them share or show what they made.

Creativity can mean many things. It doesn’t only apply to professional artists.

## Brand or website example

LEGO Ideas. On the page about LEGO Ideas, LEGO states that this is the site where fans can submit their product ideas. If the community likes the idea enough or if the idea wins a challenge, it can be produced by LEGO as an official LEGO set. In my opinion, it is Creator-type, because users can work on the idea and present it to the community.

## Application to web design

For a website about a creative pursuit, a couple of illustrations may be provided along with instructions on how to make something similar yourself. Such a site could include a "Create your own" button, a small list of instructions, and an opportunity to save or send out your creation. All of the above is merely a suggestion for consideration.
